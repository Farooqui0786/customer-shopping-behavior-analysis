# 🛍️ Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

**Turning customer shopping data into actionable business insights.**

An end-to-end data analytics project exploring customer purchasing patterns, product performance, discounts, and subscription behavior to support better retail business decisions.

## 🔄 Project Workflow

`Python → SQL → Power BI → Business Insights`

- 🐍 **Python:** Data cleaning, preprocessing, and exploratory data analysis.
- 🗄️ **SQL:** Answering business questions about customers, spending, and product performance.
- 📊 **Power BI:** Visualizing trends and presenting insights through dashboards.

## 🎯 What I Analyzed

<details>
<summary>🛒 Customer Purchasing Behavior</summary>

- Spending patterns across customer segments.
- Product categories and purchasing preferences.
- Customer demographics and purchase frequency.

</details>

<details>
<summary>💰 Sales & Marketing Analysis</summary>

- Discount and promotional code usage.
- Subscriber versus non-subscriber behavior.
- Revenue trends and product performance.

</details>

<details>
<summary>📈 Business Applications</summary>

- Identify opportunities to improve customer retention.
- Evaluate promotional strategies.
- Support data-driven marketing and sales decisions.

</details>

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

`Python` `Pandas` `SQL` `SQLAlchemy` `Power BI` `Jupyter Notebook`

## 💡 Project Outcome

Demonstrates how raw customer shopping data can be transformed into meaningful insights for understanding purchasing behavior and improving business decisions.
